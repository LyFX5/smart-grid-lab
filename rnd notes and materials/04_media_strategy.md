# Media Strategy

Goal: convert technical progress into trust, opportunities, and collaborations.

## Positioning

- Core message: Smart Grid Lab is a practical path from demo stand to digital twin to research platform.
- Differentiator: reproducible scenario-based experimentation with clear architecture.
- Tone: transparent, technical, iterative.

## Target audiences

- Researchers and university labs
- Microgrid engineers and integrators
- Applied modeling/prototyping clients (e.g., Upwork prospects)
- Potential collaborators in hydrogen/EMS/forecasting

## Knowledge assets and how to use them

- `README.md`
  - Public front page of credibility.
  - Update when capabilities or architecture meaningfully change.
- `01_roadmap.md`
  - Strategic narrative ("what is next and why").
  - Source for milestone posts and planning discussions.
- `02_decisions_log.md`
  - Shows engineering judgment and tradeoffs.
  - Source for "why we chose X over Y" posts.
- `03_experiment_log.md`
  - Evidence base for claims.
  - Source for "experiment of the week" content.

## Content pipeline (repeatable)

1. Pick one item from experiment or decision log.
2. Convert to a short story:
   - problem,
   - approach,
   - result,
   - next step.
3. Publish in one of three formats:
   - short post (LinkedIn),
   - technical note (README update or note excerpt),
   - portfolio snippet (Upwork proposal asset).
4. Link to repository and invite feedback/collaboration.

## Weekly cadence (lightweight)

- 1 technical progress update (LinkedIn)
- 1 architecture/decision post every 2 weeks
- 1 portfolio refresh for freelance applications per month

## Post structure template

- Hook: concrete project problem
- Build: what you implemented this week
- Evidence: key output/figure/metric
- Reflection: what changed in architecture understanding
- Call: who should comment/connect/test

## Calls-to-action you can rotate

- "If you work on microgrid control or forecasting, what scenario should I benchmark next?"
- "Looking for 2-3 practitioners to review assumptions in this setup."
- "Open to pilot discussions for research labs and integrator teams."

## Risk handling in communication

- Be explicit that project is early-stage and iterative.
- Emphasize reproducibility and learning velocity.
- Never overclaim validation level.
